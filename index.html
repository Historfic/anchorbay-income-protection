Questions people ask

What does income protection actually do?+

If sickness or an injury stops you working, it pays you a monthly amount, typically up to around three-quarters of what you earn. That keeps the rent or mortgage and the everyday bills covered while you get back on your feet.

Can I get cover if I work for myself?+

Yes. People who are self-employed often need it the most, since there’s no employer sick leave to lean on. Jordan regularly sets up cover for sole traders, contractors and small-business owners.

What will it cost me?+

That comes down to your age, earnings, occupation and health. Working it out is the whole point of the free chat: Jordan finds a level of cover that fits your budget.

Is the advice genuinely free?+

It is. You pay nothing and you’re under no obligation. If you choose to take out a policy, the insurer pays the adviser, not you.
